01 / OVERVIEW

A club experience,
translated to the web.

M&Y Fitness Club brings training, coaching, classes and recovery together in Vaishali Nagar, Jaipur. The website makes that range easy to explore, from the first impression to a membership enquiry.

The project balances cinematic fitness imagery with short, clear copy and a straightforward path to visiting the club.

02 / CHALLENGE

Premium appeal.
A clear way in.

The brief called for a premium fitness presence that would welcome beginners and experienced gym users alike. Visitors needed to quickly understand the club, its Jaipur location, the services offered and how to enquire.

The experience also needed to work comfortably on smaller screens while using verified business information and authentic imagery. No invented prices, trainer credentials, reviews or transformation results.

03 / SOLUTION

Strong visuals.
Thoughtful structure.

A dark, cinematic design pairs bold typography with the club’s orange brand accent. Training spaces and coaching appear through real club photography, supported by dedicated facility, class, membership and contact pages.

Trial and enquiry actions sit at natural decision points. The form validates contact details and prepares an email or directs visitors to the existing club booking page; direct CRM delivery is ready for configuration.

Accessible controls, reduced-motion support and responsive WebP images make the experience practical as well as visual.

05 / RESPONSIVE DESIGN

The same ambition.
Every screen.

Layouts adapt from 320px mobile screens to larger desktops. Cards stack, headlines scale, navigation becomes a compact menu and primary actions stay easy to tap.

  • Responsive image sizes and preserved aspect ratios
  • Mobile-first contact and trial actions
  • Keyboard navigation and visible focus states
  • Reduced-motion preferences respected
